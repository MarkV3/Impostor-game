About this trainer

A practice tool for our conservative beginner playbook, not a poker solver. The supplied opponent reads are part of each question. It covers the playbook’s decision families, not every possible poker situation. 4–9 players, fixed blinds, normally 75–150 BB. No real money, accounts, or network needed. Progress stays in this browser when browser storage is available.

Keyboard: 1–5 choose an answer · Enter next · H hint. Money is fictional.
